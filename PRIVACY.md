# Privacy at Longview Commons

Privacy is part of the structure of Longview Commons, not merely one of the topics we discuss.

We will collect as little personal information as reasonably possible and use it only for clearly explained purposes.

## Our Commitments

Longview Commons will not:

- Sell personal information
- Trade personal information
- Give sponsors access to private community data
- Publish names, email addresses, or private responses without permission
- Require unnecessary personal information for participation
- Use hidden personal profiling as a business model

## Email Communications

People may contact Longview Commons through:

**longviewcommons@gmail.com**

Email messages may contain personal information provided by the sender.

Please do not send:

- Passwords
- Account credentials
- Social Security numbers
- Banking or payment details
- Private medical records
- Government identification numbers
- Information that could put you or another person at risk

We will use submitted information only to:

- Read and respond to messages
- Understand public needs
- Improve Longview Commons resources
- Contact someone about a contribution they offered
- Request clarification or permission where necessary

## Responses to the First Question

When responding to:

[What System in Your Life Do You Depend on but Do Not Truly Control?](FIRST-QUESTION.md)

Please state whether:

- Your response may be quoted publicly with your name
- Your response may be quoted anonymously
- Your response must remain private

Unless clear permission is given, we will treat the response as private and will not publish it.

## Public Contributions

Information posted publicly through GitHub issues, discussions, pull requests, commits, or other public systems should be assumed to be visible to anyone.

Do not post sensitive personal information in public contribution channels.

Contributors should remove personal or identifying information from examples whenever it is not necessary.

## Data Minimization

As Longview Commons develops, we will prefer systems that allow:

- Participation without unnecessary accounts
- Pseudonymous participation where practical
- Export of personal data
- Deletion requests
- Privacy-respecting analytics
- Clear consent before publication
- Separation of public contributions from private communications

## Service Providers

Longview Commons currently uses third-party services including Gmail and GitHub.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and may collect technical or account information independently of Longview Commons.

We cannot promise that third-party platforms collect no information. Part of our long-term work will be reducing dependence on any single provider and creating more privacy-respecting communication options.

## Security

We will take reasonable steps to protect information entrusted to us, but no online system can guarantee complete security.

People should share only the information necessary for the purpose of their communication.

If we become aware that private information has been exposed, lost, or accessed improperly, we will make reasonable efforts to inform affected people and reduce further harm.

## Deletion Requests

A person may ask us to delete private information they previously submitted by contacting:

**longviewcommons@gmail.com**

We will make reasonable efforts to locate and delete the information unless it must be retained for legal, security, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Information already published publicly, copied by others, included in repository history, or preserved by external archives may not be fully removable.

## Future Changes

This privacy statement will evolve as Longview Commons adds communication tools, websites, donation systems, mailing lists, or community spaces.

Any significant change should be documented publicly.

## Current Status

Longview Commons is in its earliest formation stage.

At present, we are not operating:

- A membership database
- A donation system
- A mailing list
- User accounts
- Advertising trackers
- A community forum

This page describes our present practices and the principles that will guide future systems.
