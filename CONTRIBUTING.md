# Contributing to Longview Commons

Longview Commons is in its earliest formation stage.

We are not currently asking people to make major commitments. We are looking for thoughtful participation, useful criticism, practical experience, and help identifying what should be built.

## Ways to Contribute

You can help by:

- Sharing a system you depend on but do not control
- Describing a problem ordinary households face
- Pointing to an existing open-source or local-first solution
- Explaining why a current alternative is too difficult, expensive, or unreliable
- Reviewing our mission and identifying weaknesses
- Testing future guides
- Improving documentation
- Translating technical ideas into plain language
- Offering relevant technical, organizational, or practical knowledge
- Suggesting a project that would create broad public value

## Participation Expectations

Contributors should:

- Act in good faith
- Treat others with respect
- Be honest about uncertainty
- Avoid unnecessary political or ideological conflict
- Focus criticism on ideas, systems, and evidence
- Protect private and sensitive information
- Disclose important conflicts of interest
- Avoid presenting untested claims as established fact
- Consider long-term effects, not only immediate convenience
- Help make resources understandable to ordinary people

## Public Contributions

Longview Commons has not yet selected its permanent licensing structure.

Before submitting substantial original writing, code, designs, or other materials, please contact us so expectations about attribution, licensing, ownership, and future use are clear.

Small corrections, suggestions, issue reports, and links to existing resources are welcome.

## Current Priorities

Our present priorities are:

1. Listen to individuals and families
2. Identify common fragile dependencies
3. Find existing solutions worth documenting
4. Design the Practical Independence Assessment
5. Create useful public resources
6. Find trustworthy contributors who share the mission

## What We Are Not Looking For

At this stage, we are not seeking:

- Partisan campaigning
- Attacks on individuals or groups
- Fear-based speculation without practical value
- Hidden commercial promotion
- Attempts to control the project through money or status
- Personal information that could put someone at risk
- Large commitments before trust has been established
